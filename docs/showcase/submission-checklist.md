Deadlines below are transcribed from the supplied Fall 2026 course guidance. Eastern Time is the official time zone; confirm all details against the pinned course deliverables guide.

| Deliverable | Deadline (Eastern Time) | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Poster PDF | Monday, November 30, 2026, 5:00 PM | One page; portrait 36 x 48 in recommended, or landscape 48 x 36 in; upload to My Showcase. |

The supplied guidance says teams of 8 or more may use double video limits. The project plan says this team has four students, so standard limits apply unless the instructor confirms otherwise.

## YouTube checklist - repeat for all four videos

- [ ] Upload as **Public** or **Unlisted**, never Private.
- [ ] Set audience to **Not Made for Kids**.
- [ ] Captions are enabled and reviewed for names and technical terms.
- [ ] Video is 1080p.
- [ ] Replace `[TEAM]` with the official team name in these titles:
  - `Fall 2026 - [TEAM] - Intro`
  - `Fall 2026 - [TEAM] - Demo`
  - `Fall 2026 - [TEAM] - Shortcomings`
  - `Fall 2026 - [TEAM] - Maintenance`
- [ ] Paste each link into the required My Showcase field and verify it opens.

## Content and participation

- [ ] Intro covers problem, users, solution, team names on screen, and confirmed Product Owner/sponsor.
- [ ] Each of the four team members explains their own contribution in one video or the live showcase.
- [ ] Demo starts with the strongest actual result, narrates one use case start-to-finish, then explains next work.
- [ ] Demo calls the RAM torn-write case a simulation, not a physical power cut.
- [ ] Prepare an actual local demo/recording fallback in case Zoom fails.
- [ ] Shortcomings video explains what does not work yet, what comes next and why, plus responsible AI use.
- [ ] Installation video explains repository location, clean-machine setup, configuration without secrets, CI, and maintenance.

## My Showcase and event

- [ ] Upload one-page poster PDF by the poster deadline.
- [ ] Upload slides, project information, and required documents.
- [ ] Enter and test both real Zoom room links with a teammate.
- [ ] Attend Rehearsal 1 or makeup.
- [ ] Keep screenshots/confirmation and copies of final uploaded files/links.

## External actions not completed by repo files

These files do not record videos, turn on captions, publish to YouTube, upload to My Showcase, create Zoom rooms, or attend rehearsal. Mark those tasks complete only after the team performs and verifies each action. Team/sponsor names and individual contributions must be confirmed, not guessed.
